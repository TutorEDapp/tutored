Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version. We collect only what we need to run lessons. We never sell your data or show you ads. Students under 18 need a parent's consent before they can use TutorED. You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to delete it, at any time.

1. Who we are

TutorED is an online tutoring platform for students in Sri Lanka. It connects students with verified tutors for live lessons on Google Meet, homework and study packs. "TutorED", "we" and "us" in this policy mean the people who run the platform. We decide how your personal data is used, so we are responsible for it under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022.

This policy covers our website and dashboards. It explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do.

2. Information we collect

Students:

  • Your name, email address and password. We never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Your study level and age, and your parent or guardian's email if you are under 18.
  • The tutors you save, the lessons you book, the study packs you add, and the messages, homework and files you send.

Tutors:

  • Your name, email, password, subjects, hourly rate, teaching languages, short bio, profile photo and qualifications.
  • Your ID and qualification documents, used to verify you.
  • Your open times, lessons, messages, homework, marking and files.

Parents and guardians:

  • Your email address, and if you give consent, your name, your relationship to the student, your choices and the date and time.

Anyone who contacts us:

  • Your name, email and what you write to us.

Technical information:

  • Your browser keeps a login token so you stay signed in.
  • Our hosting and service providers keep short-term logs, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site working and secure.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking tools.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account, and to keep it secure.
  • To show tutors' profiles and open times, and to book, change or cancel lessons.
  • To create a Google Calendar invitation with a Google Meet link for each lesson.
  • To let students and tutors message each other and share homework and files.
  • To check tutors' identity and qualifications before they can teach.
  • To ask a parent or guardian for consent, and to record their answer, when a student is under 18.
  • To send emails you need, such as lesson invitations, consent requests and confirmations. We only send update emails to parents who agree to them.
  • To answer your messages, handle complaints and refunds, and meet our legal obligations.

We use your data because it is needed to provide the service you signed up for, because you (or your parent) consented, because the law requires it, or because it is needed to keep TutorED safe. We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising or make automated decisions about you.

TutorED does not record lessons. Please don't record a lesson unless everyone in it agrees.

4. Who can see your information

  • Students see a verified tutor's public profile: name, photo, subjects, rate, languages, bio, qualifications and open times.
  • Tutors see the name of each student who books them, the lesson details, and the messages, homework and files shared with them.
  • TutorED admins can see accounts, documents, bookings and messages when needed to verify tutors, give support, keep students safe or deal with a complaint.
  • Tutors' ID and qualification documents are never shown to students.

We use these service providers to run TutorED:

  • Supabase: login, database and file storage.
  • Google: Calendar and Meet for lessons, Gmail for our emails, and the fonts used on our pages.
  • GitHub: website hosting.

They only process data to provide their service to us. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

5. Tutor ID and qualification documents

Tutors upload an ID document and proof of qualifications so we can verify them. These files are stored privately. Only the tutor and TutorED admins can open them. We use them only to verify the tutor and to deal with any later complaint.

If a tutor is not approved, we delete their documents within 90 days. Approved tutors' documents are kept while the tutor teaches on TutorED.

6. Payments

Payments are switched off during our trial, so we don't collect any payment details at the moment.

When payments start, they will be handled by a licensed payment provider. You enter your card or bank details on the provider's secure page, and TutorED never sees or stores your full card number. We keep a record of each payment (amount, date, lesson and status), which we need for refunds, paying tutors and tax.

7. Children's privacy and parental consent

Many of our students are school pupils. Sri Lankan law treats anyone under 16 as a child. We go further: every student under 18 needs a parent or guardian's consent before they can use TutorED.

  • When a student under 18 signs up, we ask for their parent or guardian's email. This must be different from the student's own email.
  • We email the parent a private link, valid for 7 days. The parent can see what we collect and why, and choose to give or refuse consent.
  • Until consent is given, the account is locked. The student can only see and edit their own profile. They cannot browse tutors, book lessons, send messages or upload work, and tutors cannot message them.
  • If a parent prefers, our team can take consent by phone and record it.
  • A parent can withdraw consent at any time using the link in their confirmation email, or by contacting us. The account is then locked again. If the parent asks, we delete it.

We only collect what is needed for lessons. We never use children's data for marketing.

8. Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. We do store a login token in your browser, so you stay signed in. If you sign out or clear your browser data, it is removed.

Our pages load fonts from Google. This means your browser contacts Google's servers, which see your IP address.

9. How long we keep data

  • Your account and profile: while you use TutorED. If you delete your account (Account settings → Delete my account), your profile is removed straight away. Your login, files and messages are deleted within 30 days.
  • Lesson and payment records: 6 years after the lesson, because we need them for tax and accounting.
  • Unapproved tutors' ID and qualification documents: deleted within 90 days.
  • Parental consent records: 3 years after the student turns 18 or the account closes, whichever comes first, so we can show consent was given.
  • Messages sent through our contact form: up to 2 years.
  • Backups may hold deleted data for a short time before they are overwritten.

10. Where your data is stored and how we protect it

Our service providers store data on servers that may be outside Sri Lanka. We only use well-known providers that protect data under contract.

How we protect it:

  • All traffic to TutorED is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Database rules limit each person to the data they are allowed to see.
  • Documents and homework files are stored privately.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form.
  • Parents' consent links are stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form.

If a data breach could harm you, we will tell the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka. Where the risk to you is high, we will tell you as soon as we can.

11. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is wrong or incomplete. You can change most of it yourself in Account settings or Profile & settings.
  • Delete your data, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Stop using your data for a purpose you object to.
  • Withdraw consent you gave. A parent can do this for a child.

To make a request, email us at the address below. We may ask you to prove who you are before we act. We'll reply within one month, free of charge. If we need longer, we'll tell you within that month; the extra time will be no more than two months.

If you're unhappy with how we handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka (dpa.gov.lk).

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our service or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will tell you by email or on your dashboard before it takes effect. If the change affects what a parent agreed to, we will ask the parent again.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email [Add privacy email], or use our Contact page. Please put "Privacy" in the subject line.

          The big picture

          The website you see is the front end. It shows pages and collects what people type. It never decides who is allowed to do what. That job belongs to the back end: a server that checks every request, saves data in a database, and talks to outside services like SMS, payments and email.

          What each part does

          Sign-in service
          Handles email and password, Google sign-in and phone codes. Stores passwords safely (scrambled, never readable) and tells the server who each person is.
          API server
          The rule-keeper. "Only admins can approve tutors." "A student can only see their own bookings." Every request is checked here.
          Database
          Tables of records: users, tutor profiles, bookings, payments, messages. This dashboard reads from it.
          File storage
          Private storage for ID cards and certificates. Only admins can open them, through short-lived links.
          SMS provider
          Sends the 6-digit login codes to phones.
          Payment gateway
          Takes card payments. Your server never sees the full card number. The gateway confirms each payment back to the server.
          Email service
          Sends welcome emails, booking confirmations and "your profile is approved" notices.

          When a tutor signs up

          1. The tutor fills in the form. The sign-in service creates their account.
          2. Their ID and certificates go to private file storage.
          3. The server saves a tutor profile with status Waiting.
          4. The profile appears in your Tutor verification queue.
          5. You check the documents and approve, reject or ask for more.
          6. The server records your decision, updates the status, and emails the tutor.
          7. Approved tutors appear in student search with a verified badge.
